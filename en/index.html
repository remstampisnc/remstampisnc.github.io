Our company

ABOUT US

REM Stampi snc was founded in 1987 by brothers Eugenio and Roberto Miazzi, with the mission of producing high-quality aluminium moulds for the industrial sector.

Over the years the company has increasingly specialised in the design and manufacture of moulds, continuously investing in technology and training. Today it relies on 4 CNC machining centres, one of which has 5 axes, state-of-the-art CAD-CAM software and a highly qualified technical team.

We offer a complete and personalised service: from project feasibility assessment and 3D design through to mould manufacture and production start-up.

What we offer

OUR SERVICES

We offer a complete range of services for the design and manufacture of aluminium moulds, from the initial concept through to after-sales support.

CAD CAM 3D Design

Advanced design with state-of-the-art software for maximum precision and product optimisation.

Mould Manufacturing

Manufacture of aluminium moulds with CNC machining up to 5 axes for centesimal tolerances.

Laser Scanning & Reverse Engineering

3D acquisition of existing components for faithful replicas or modifications.

Maintenance & Assistance

Preventive and corrective maintenance service to extend the useful life of moulds.

Testing & Quality Control

Dimensional verification and functional testing to ensure conformity of the finished product.

Prototyping & Resin Models

Prototypes and resin models to validate the design before full production.
